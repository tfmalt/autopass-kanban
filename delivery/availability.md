# Project Availability

This calendar records project-wide hiatuses, public holidays, vacation, and other planned absence. Dates are inclusive. `Availability` is the remaining capacity during the entry; use `Who = *` for team-wide `holiday` and `hiatus` entries, and a team member email for `vacation` and `absence` entries.
